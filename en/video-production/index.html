Special rate for Christian ministries

If you represent a ministry, church or Christian organization, you may receive a 30% discount on Truth Frame Studio’s professional fees. The discount is confirmed once we review the project and does not apply to travel, locations, rentals, special equipment or third-party costs.

How a production is paid

Each production receives a custom quote. Once the proposal is approved, the recommended payment schedule is the same for content production and special productions.

  • 50 % to book and begin.
  • 30 % before filming.
  • 20 % before final delivery.

We film in Santo Domingo in blocks, or bring the crew to your city. Travel, accommodation, equipment transport, permits and local production costs are quoted separately.
