    The idea first. Then the camera.

    We don’t guess what keeps an audience watching: we design it in the script, shape it on set and refine it in the edit. When we review a channel, we work on four fronts.

    Packaging that earns the click

    Titles that raise a question and thumbnails tested in A, B and C versions. If nobody clicks, the best video doesn’t exist.

    A hook in the first seconds

    We open with the strongest line in the content so no one leaves in the first minute, without changing what makes the format work.

    Shorts that lead to the full episode

    We turn the best moments in your archive into Shorts and Reels designed to bring new viewers to the long episodes.

    An audience you own

    Relying only on YouTube is building on rented land. We design ways to turn viewers into contacts you own.

    The authority behind the lens

    We’re not a marketing agency trying to make videos. We’re filmmakers and digital strategists who figured out how retention works on YouTube.

    Behind the studio are Andrés Figueroa and Diana Cardona, a married couple who have worked together in video production since 1999. They bring more than 25 years of audiovisual experience and have worked on channels in Colombia, the United States and the Dominican Republic.

    It all started at university, with a documentary about a missionary and a corporate video for a clinic. Several years of wedding films followed, before the projects that back their experience today.

    Frequently asked questions

    Don’t see your question? Message us on WhatsApp.

    Do you work with clients outside the Dominican Republic?

    Yes. You can come to Santo Domingo and batch-record a full season, or we send our film crew to your offices or any location you choose, anywhere in the world. Travel is quoted separately.

    Do I need to have a channel to work with you?

    No. With the authority incubator we build your channel from scratch. And if you don’t yet have a high-value product or service to sell, we start by designing it with you in the offer incubator.

    How long does each service take?

    An audit’s master plan takes 2 weeks, and its implementation, 3 months. The authority incubator takes 4 weeks, and the offer incubator, 2 to 3. Strategic implementation takes 4 to 6 weeks, and full implementation starts with a 3-month commitment.

    What do the prices include?

    The prices we publish are starting points. The final proposal depends on the scope of the project, and travel, locations, talent and other external costs are quoted separately.

    Can my own team shoot the content?

    Yes. The 10 scripts from the authority incubator can be shot by your team or ours. And with strategic implementation, we design the system so your team can run it on its own.

    Why is there a viability interview?

    Because an authority channel only works if there is real knowledge and experience behind it. If we see the model isn’t sustainable in the long run, we’d rather tell you before we start.

    Does my channel need to make money from AdSense?

    No. We don’t design channels to rely on YouTube ads; we design them to bring in clients, with every video leading your audience toward your offer.

    What’s the first step?

    Send us your inquiry through the form on this page. Diana will reply personally within 24 hours and, depending on what you need, we’ll agree on the next step together. Asking questions and requesting a proposal is free.
