    Refreshing the existing catalog

    For existing channels, the strategy includes reviving older content. The workload is set by the highest criterion the channel meets. For each selected older video we can optimize the title, thumbnail, description, end screen, cards and matching playlist.

    A/B/C variants are reserved for the older videos with the most potential; the rest get one optimized final thumbnail. For new channels, that time goes into building the channel structure and new uploads.

    Why a six-month minimum?

    A channel needs time, continuity and enough published content to generate useful data about how its audience responds. The first month lets us set the starting point; the following months let us produce consistently, spot patterns and decide with more representative data.

    The six-month commitment protects your investment from hasty conclusions based on one or two isolated videos. At the end, we’ll review the work with you and decide whether to continue, adjust the scope or hand the operation over to your team.

    Shooting is easy. Making it work is another story.

    Anyone can shoot and publish a video. The real challenge is creating content that earns the click, holds attention, builds trust and delivers results.

    A good camera isn’t enough for that. It takes understanding the audience, reading the data, finding the right idea, building a solid narrative and presenting every video in a way that sparks interest from the very first moment.

    At Truth Frame Studio we bring together strategy, analysis and video production so every piece of content has a clear purpose: expanding your reach, strengthening your authority and turning your audience’s attention into real opportunities.

    For more than 25 years we have directed and produced projects for television, brands, organizations and digital platforms. That experience lets us see what each project needs and build the right process to move it forward.

    We bring the method, the ideas, the strategy and the technical direction. You bring the knowledge, the experience and the voice that only you can take in front of the camera. Together, we turn them into content that goes further.

    We operate from Santo Domingo, with our corporate and legal structure in the Dominican Republic.

    Communication that builds value and trust

    Truth Frame means the truth inside the lens. Our name stands for the conviction that a production can be strategic, compelling and technically excellent without giving up honesty.

    We are humans working for humans. Technology expands what we can do, but it’s judgment, proven experience and professional responsibility that support every decision.

    That’s why we choose to work with people and organizations that have a clear vision, a legitimate purpose and an awareness of the impact they can make. We look for projects that inform, educate, build trust, preserve stories or bring something of value to society.

    If you’re an experienced professional with real knowledge, and you know your experience can add value to others, we want to help you communicate it with clarity, excellence and authenticity.

    If you represent an organization committed to making a positive impact, we want to turn its vision into content that strengthens its credibility, expands its reach and connects with the people who need to know it.

    We believe trust is built when there is truth behind the message. You bring the experience, the vision and the purpose; we bring the method, the ideas and the technical direction to put them on screen.

    Frequently asked questions

    Don’t see your question? Message us on WhatsApp.

    What’s the first step?

    Send us your inquiry through the form on this page. Diana will reply personally within 24 hours and, depending on what you need, we’ll agree on the next step together. Asking questions and requesting a proposal is free.

    Do you work with clients outside the Dominican Republic?

    Yes. We can work with people, brands and organizations in any country. Strategy, audit, planning and management services can be done entirely online.

    When the project includes video production, you can travel to Santo Domingo for batch recording days that produce several weeks or months of content, or our team can travel to your city or country. When travel is needed, flights, lodging, equipment transport, permits and local production costs are quoted separately.

    Before we start, we’ll work out with you which option best protects your project’s quality, schedule and investment.

    Do I need to have a channel to work with you?

    No. With the YouTube channel launch we build your channel from scratch, and the Strategy session also works if your channel is still an idea. The audit, on the other hand, is designed for channels that already have content and data to analyze.

    How long does each service take?

    Timelines depend on the scope of the service and start once we have received the corresponding payment, the completed form and all the necessary materials. As a guide:

    • Strategy session: the meeting lasts two hours. The report and roadmap are delivered within 48 hours.
    • Strategy audit: 10 business days.
    • Strategic launch: about four weeks.
    • Full-service launch: about six to eight weeks.
    • Strategic or full-service management: monthly cycles, with an initial six-month minimum.
    • Special video productions: the schedule depends on the scope, shooting days, locations and complexity of the project.

    Before we start, you’ll receive a clear calendar with the planned stages and dates.

    How do payments work?

    The payment schedule depends on the scope of the service:

    • The Strategy session and the Standard audit are paid in full when booking.
    • The Institutional audit is split into two payments: 50% to start and 50% before the delivery session.
    • The Strategic launch is split into two payments: 50% to start and 50% before final delivery.
    • The Full-service launch and special productions are split into three stages: 50% to book, 30% before shooting and 20% before you receive the final files.
    • Monthly management services are paid in advance at the start of each month.

    Travel and third-party services are paid before the corresponding bookings are confirmed. Before we start, you’ll receive a proposal that clearly sets out the scope, the calendar and the payment schedule.

    How does the initial fit call work?

    It’s a free online conversation of up to 30 minutes that we offer after reviewing your form. It applies to the Institutional audit, the Channel launch, Monthly management and Special video productions. The Strategy session and the Standard audit can be booked directly.

    Its purpose is to confirm that we can help with what you need, understand the overall scope of the project and point you to the right service. It doesn’t include an audit, detailed recommendations or deliverables. When there’s a good fit, you receive a proposal with the scope, the investment and the next steps.

    What do you need from me to get started?

    We’ll ask for the information we need to understand your project. Depending on the service, it may include:

    • Information about your business, organization or personal brand.
    • The project’s goals, audience and priorities.
    • Your channel link and relevant metrics, where applicable.
    • Visual identity, logos and other brand materials.
    • Availability for meetings, shoots and approvals.
    • One person in charge of centralizing communication and decisions, when we work with an organization.

    You don’t need to have everything figured out before contacting us: we’ll tell you exactly which materials we need and how to share them. We’ll never ask for personal passwords; when we need to work on a platform, we’ll use the team permissions and access it offers.

    Do you guarantee a specific number of views or subscribers?

    We don’t promise a specific number of views, subscribers or sales. Doing so wouldn’t be honest, because results also depend on how the audience responds, the competition, the project’s consistency, the market and how each piece of content performs.

    What you can expect is a team with proven experience, decisions grounded in real data, professional production, ongoing follow-up and clear direction for your channel. The TFS System is designed to reduce improvisation and give every piece of content better conditions to generate reach, trust and opportunities.

    Does Truth Frame Studio take on every project?

    We review every request before confirming a collaboration. To protect the integrity of the work, the trust between both sides and the quality of the result, we accept projects that are compatible with our ethical, professional and institutional principles.

    We reserve the right to decline projects whose purpose, message, practices or content conflict with our values, with human dignity, with applicable law or with our professional standards. A collaboration is confirmed once the scope is approved, the proposal is accepted and the corresponding payment is made.

    Does my channel need to make money from AdSense?

    No. We don’t design channels to rely on YouTube ads; we design them to bring in clients, with every video leading your audience toward your offer.
