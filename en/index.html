    To communicate is to turn a vision into meaning.

    A vision can be clear to the person who built it, but it still needs the right form for other people to understand it, remember it and find value in it.

    Since 1999 we have helped turn ideas, knowledge and stories into video content and experiences that faithfully represent what each person, brand or organization wants to say.

    At Truth Frame Studio we combine strategy, data, direction and video production to develop YouTube channels, content, digital ecosystems and live events with a clear purpose.

    Our experience lets us interpret each client’s vision and translate it into communication that is professional, honest and meaningful to their audience.

    You bring the knowledge, the experience and the vision. We bring the method, the ideas, the strategy and the direction to give them a form your audience can understand, remember and value.

    We operate from Santo Domingo and work on projects in the Dominican Republic and abroad.

    Communication that builds value and trust

    Truth Frame means the truth inside the lens. Our name stands for the conviction that a production can be strategic, compelling and technically excellent without giving up honesty.

    We are humans working for humans. Technology expands what we can do, but it’s judgment, proven experience and professional responsibility that support every decision.

    That’s why we choose to work with people and organizations that have a clear vision, a legitimate purpose and an awareness of the impact they can make. We look for projects that inform, educate, build trust, preserve stories or bring something of value to society.

    If you’re an experienced professional with real knowledge, and you know your experience can add value to others, we want to help you communicate it with clarity, excellence and authenticity.

    If you represent an organization committed to making a positive impact, we want to turn its vision into content that strengthens its credibility, expands its reach and connects with the people who need to know it.

    We believe trust is built when there is truth behind the message. You bring the experience, the vision and the purpose; we bring the method, the ideas and the technical direction to put them on screen.

    What’s the first step?

    Send us your inquiry through the form on this page. Diana will reply personally within 24 hours and, depending on what you need, we’ll agree on the next step together. Asking questions and requesting a proposal is free.

    What exactly does Truth Frame Studio do?

    We are a strategy and video production studio. Our work is organized into four divisions:

    • YouTube: strategy session, channel launch from scratch and management of an existing channel.
    • Video production: content produced in blocks, and special productions.
    • Content and digital media agency: media direction or full management of the ecosystem.
    • Live events: production and streaming, or full event production.
    Do you work with clients outside the Dominican Republic?

    Yes. We can work with people, brands and organizations anywhere. Strategy, planning and management services can run entirely online.

    When the project includes video production, you can travel to Santo Domingo for batch recording days that produce several weeks or months of content, or our team can travel to your city or country. When travel is needed, flights, lodging, equipment transport, permits and local production costs are quoted separately.

    Before we start, we’ll work out with you which option best protects your project’s quality, schedule and investment.

    Do I need to have a channel to work with you?

    No. With the Channel launch we build and produce your channel from scratch, and the Strategy session also works when your channel is still an idea. And if what you need doesn’t involve YouTube, the Video production and Live events divisions work project by project.

    Can you handle just my Instagram, Facebook or TikTok?

    No. Our agency service treats YouTube as the main platform of the ecosystem and, from there, directs the other digital channels so they widen the content’s reach and keep the same direction.

    We don’t offer individual networks or standalone packages, and we don’t work as a community manager for a single platform. That decision protects the coherence of the message and the result of the project.

    How long does each service take?

    Timelines depend on the scope of the service and start once we have received the corresponding payment, the completed form and all the necessary materials. As a guide:

    • Strategy session: the meeting lasts two hours. The report and roadmap are delivered within 48 hours.
    • Channel launch: six to eight weeks.
    • Channel management and growth: monthly cycles, with an initial commitment of at least six months. The first month is a full assessment and the definition of priorities.
    • Digital media direction or management: monthly cycles, with an initial six-month commitment.
    • Content production, special productions and events: the schedule depends on the scope, the shooting days, the locations and the complexity of the project.

    Before we start, you’ll receive a clear calendar with the planned stages and dates.

    How do payments work?

    How you pay depends on the service:

    • The Strategy session is paid in full on booking.
    • Monthly services are paid in advance at the start of each month.
    • Content production and Special productions are paid 50 % to book, 30 % before filming and 20 % before final delivery.
    • The Channel launch is paid 50 % to begin, 30 % before filming and 20 % before release.
    • Events are paid 50 % to begin, 30 % before the event and 20 % before the final content is delivered.

    Third-party costs — venue rental, extra equipment, set design, external sound and lighting, talent, travel, accommodation, transport, permits, security and special logistics — are paid before each booking is confirmed. Before we begin, you’ll receive a proposal with the scope, the schedule and the payment split clearly set out.

    Is the Strategy session credited if I hire another service afterwards?

    Yes. If you hire a channel launch or management service within 15 days of the session, we credit 60 % of what you paid for it.

    What do you need from me to get started?

    We’ll ask for the information we need to understand your project. Depending on the service, it may include:

    • Information about your business, organization or personal brand.
    • The project’s goals, audience and priorities.
    • Your channel link and relevant metrics, where applicable.
    • Visual identity, logos and other brand materials.
    • Availability for meetings, shoots and approvals.
    • One person in charge of centralizing communication and decisions, when we work with an organization.

    You don’t need to have everything figured out before contacting us: we’ll tell you exactly which materials we need and how to share them. We’ll never ask for personal passwords; when we need to work on a platform, we’ll use the team permissions and access it offers.

    Do you guarantee a specific number of views or subscribers?

    We don’t promise a specific number of views, subscribers or sales. Doing so wouldn’t be honest, because results also depend on how the audience responds, the competition, the project’s consistency, the market and how each piece of content performs.

    What you can expect is a team with proven experience, decisions grounded in real information, professional production, continuous follow-up and a clear direction. We work with a system of our own, backed by data, experience and an understanding of the audience, designed to reduce guesswork and give each piece of content better conditions to generate reach, trust and opportunities.

    Does Truth Frame Studio take on every project?

    We review every request before confirming a collaboration. To protect the integrity of the work, the trust between both sides and the quality of the result, we accept projects that are compatible with our ethical, professional and institutional principles.

    We reserve the right to decline projects whose purpose, message, practices or content conflict with our values, with human dignity, with applicable law or with our professional standards. A collaboration is confirmed once the scope is approved, the proposal is accepted and the corresponding payment is made.

    Does my channel need to make money from AdSense?

    No. We don’t design channels to depend on YouTube advertising, but to widen your reach, strengthen your authority and create real opportunities for your project.
