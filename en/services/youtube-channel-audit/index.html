Your channel already has data. What you need is to turn it into decisions.

Publishing more content doesn’t always bring better results. Sometimes the problem lies in the topics you choose, the way each video is presented, audience retention or the lack of a strategy that connects all the pieces.

This audit is for you if:

  • You publish often, but the channel isn’t growing as you expected.
  • Some videos work, but you don’t know exactly why.
  • Your thumbnails get impressions, but few clicks.
  • Viewers start watching your videos, but leave too soon.
  • You don’t know which topics, titles or formats to prioritize.
  • You have a production team, but it needs clearer strategic direction.
  • You want to make decisions based on metrics, not just instinct.

It’s not about starting over. It’s about identifying what already has potential, fixing what’s holding the channel back and building a clear path for the next 90 days.

Real experience behind every recommendation

Our team doesn’t look at YouTube from theory alone. We have directed, produced and optimized content for channels that have built large audiences and sustainable production systems.

By the end of the audit, you’ll know exactly how to move forward.

You’ll get clear direction for the next 90 days and the priorities you need to better focus your team’s time, budget and work.

Our own system, applied to your channel

Our team will work on your project using the TFS System, our own methodology that connects strategy, analysis and video production.

You receive the diagnosis, the priorities and the roadmap. We take care of the process needed to build them.

The audit will help you:

  • Understand what’s currently limiting your channel’s growth.
  • Recognize which content has the most potential to reach your audience.
  • Know what to keep, what needs to change and what to stop doing.
  • Spot opportunities to revive valuable content from your catalog.
  • Make better decisions about your upcoming videos.
  • Align your team around a shared direction.
  • Move forward with a concrete plan instead of publishing on instinct.
  • Have strategic ideas ready to start executing.

How you’ll get clear direction for your channel

  1. Tell us about your project

    You’ll fill out a form with general information about your channel. Once the audit is confirmed, we’ll send you a simple guide with the data we need to get started.

    You won’t have to share passwords or give us access to your account.

  2. Diagnostic session

    We’ll meet to understand your goals, where the channel stands today and what you need to get out of the audit.

    You can invite one team member to the standard audit and up to three to the institutional audit.

  3. Applying the TFS System

    Our team will work on your project using our own methodology to build the diagnosis, the priorities and the channel’s strategic direction.

    The standard audit takes 10 business days. The institutional audit can take up to 15 business days.

  4. Delivering the results

    You’ll receive the diagnosis, the 90-day roadmap and the strategic ideas for your audit level. In the delivery session we’ll walk through the priorities so you and your team can start executing them with clarity.

    Afterwards you’ll receive a detailed report with the agreed conclusions, decisions, priorities and next steps, and you’ll have five business days to send follow-up questions by email.

Frequently asked questions

Don’t see your question? Message us on WhatsApp.

What information do I need to send for the audit?

Once you confirm, you’ll receive a form and a personalized guide with the information we need about your channel.

You won’t have to share your password or give us admin access. The data is sent securely following our instructions.

Does this audit work for a channel that doesn’t exist yet?

No. The audit is designed for channels that already have enough content and data to analyze. If you want to start from scratch, the right service is the YouTube channel launch.

Does the audit include implementing the changes?

No. You’ll receive the diagnosis, the recommendations and a roadmap ready to execute with your team.

If you need Truth Frame Studio to implement the strategy, refresh the catalog or take on production, you can continue with one of our management plans.

When will I get the results?

The standard audit is delivered in 10 business days, and the institutional audit can take up to 15 business days. The timeline starts once we receive the initial payment, the form and all the requested metrics.

Can my team take part?

Yes. In the standard audit you can invite one team member. In the institutional audit, up to three additional people can take part.

Does the audit guarantee that my channel will grow?

No responsible audit can guarantee a specific number of views or subscribers. What you will get is a data-driven diagnosis, clear priorities and a plan to make better decisions over the next 90 days.

Can you audit more than one channel?

Yes. When an organization needs several channels analyzed, we prepare a proposal based on the number of channels, their size and the complexity of the project.

Can I share the audit documents?

Reports, roadmaps and other documents can be shared with the team members involved in the project. They are for internal use and may not be published, resold or distributed externally.

Can I hire the audit if I live outside the Dominican Republic?

Yes. The audit is done entirely online, so we can analyze channels and work with clients in any country. Sessions are scheduled around the client’s time zone, and metrics are sent digitally.

Can Truth Frame Studio manage my channel if my company is in another country?

Yes. We can work as your channel’s strategy agency even if your organization and team are outside the Dominican Republic.

Strategy, data analysis, ideation, pre-production, optimization, post-production and publishing can all be handled remotely. We can also work directly with your local video team and give them creative and technical guidelines for each shoot.

How does shooting work if we’re in another country?

We can work in three ways:

  1. Production with your local team: your team shoots following our pre-production, scripts and technical direction, then sends us the footage so we can handle post-production, optimization and publishing.
  2. Batch recording in Santo Domingo: you can travel to the Dominican Republic and concentrate several weeks or months of content into one or more batch recording days. Our team handles direction and video production in Santo Domingo.
  3. Production in your country: the Truth Frame Studio team can travel to shoot in your city. In that case we’ll prepare a proposal that includes the production requirements and travel costs.

We’ll choose the most efficient option based on location, content volume and available resources.

Do prices include travel and locations?

Base prices for services that include shooting cover one production day and one location in Santo Domingo, unless the proposal says otherwise.

Flights, lodging, transportation, per diems, permits, location rental, local crew and other costs needed to produce outside Santo Domingo are quoted separately.
