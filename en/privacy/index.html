Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us on this site.

Last updated: September 21, 2026.

1. Who we are

Truth Frame Studio is a video production studio based in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. We are responsible for the information we receive through truthframestudio.com. For any matter related to your information, write to us at dicardona.studio@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

Only what you give us in the “Tell us about your project” form:

  • Your full name, the name of your project, brand or organization, and your city and country.
  • Your email address and your WhatsApp number.
  • Your answers about the type of project, the area you need help with, your project’s link and what you want to achieve.

We don’t ask for passwords, access to your accounts or payment details on this site.

3. How we use it and where it is stored

We use your information only to answer your inquiry, recommend the next step and, if you ask, prepare a proposal. By sending the form you authorize us to contact you by email or WhatsApp about that inquiry.

Inquiries are stored in a private spreadsheet in the studio’s Google account, which only our team can access, and trigger an email notice to Diana Cardona. While that register is not yet active, the form opens your own email app with your answers so you can send them to us yourself.

We keep your inquiry for as long as it is useful to serve you or for a possible collaboration. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

4. What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or share your information with third parties, or use it for advertising.
  • This site uses no cookies of its own and no advertising trackers. It currently has no analytics; if we add them, it will be a cookie-free tool and we will say so here.
  • The site’s typeface is served from our own domain, with no calls to outside services.

5. YouTube videos

Portfolio videos load from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and only when you choose to play one. Until then, your browser does not connect to YouTube. Links to YouTube and WhatsApp open in a new tab and are governed by those platforms’ policies.

6. Your rights

At any time you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Write to us at dicardona.studio@gmail.com and we’ll reply within 24 business hours.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change the way we handle your information, we will update this page and its date.
