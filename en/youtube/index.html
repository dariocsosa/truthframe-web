Channel launch from scratch

Start with a channel that represents the real value of your brand

We build and produce your channel from scratch so it starts with a professional identity, a clear proposition and enough content to connect with its audience from day one.

When we finish you receive a fully set-up, active channel, ready to begin a new stage of communication and growth.

The launch includes a Hero video and between eight and ten main pieces of content produced by Truth Frame Studio. Main content is not Shorts: short promotional clips are add-ons and are defined in the proposal.
