YouTube strategy session

Two hours to find clarity and define your channel’s next step

For professionals, brands and organizations that need to assess how YouTube can drive their project, define the next step for an existing channel or make a clear decision before investing more time and resources.

What you get

  • A prior review of the information and metrics you send us.
  • A private two-hour online session with Diana.
  • A strategic diagnosis of the channel, the content and the goals.
  • A detailed report of the meeting.
  • A roadmap with priorities for the next 30 days.
  • The option to invite someone from your team at no extra cost.

Book my session →

The report is delivered within five business days after the session. If you hire us to launch or direct your channel within the following 15 days, we credit 60% of the session fee.

Booking and preparation
  1. You make the payment.
  2. You complete the preparation form.
  3. Once we receive all the information, we give you access to the calendar.
  4. You choose the date and time.
  5. You receive your session confirmation.

To book a date, you’ll first need to complete the preparation form and share the requested information. This lets our team review your project beforehand, so the session can be spent gaining clarity, making decisions and defining the next steps.

The preparation form will ask for:

  • The link to the channel or project.
  • Your main goal.
  • The decision or problem you need to solve.
  • Screenshots or reports from YouTube Analytics for the last 90 days: overview, content and audience.
  • Your three priority questions.
  • The name and role of the team member who will attend, if any.

We will never ask for your passwords or access to your accounts.

Rescheduling and cancellation

If you need to change the date, you can reschedule the session once at no extra cost, as long as you let us know at least 24 hours in advance. That way we can set aside the time needed again to review your project and give you the preparation it requires.

You can cancel before sending the preparation form. Once we receive the information, our team begins the personalized analysis of your project; for that reason, from that point on the fee is non-refundable, although you keep the option to reschedule the session under the conditions above.
