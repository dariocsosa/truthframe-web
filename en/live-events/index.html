How an event is paid

Each event receives a custom quote. Once the proposal is approved, this is the recommended payment schedule. Truth Frame Studio’s fee is always kept separate from third-party costs.

  • 50 % to book and begin.
  • 30 % before the event.
  • 20 % before the final content is delivered.

Third-party costs include venue rental, extra technical equipment, set design, external sound and lighting, talent, travel, accommodation, transport, permits, security and special logistics. They are paid before each booking is confirmed.
